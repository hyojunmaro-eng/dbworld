DB Group

Since 1969, DB Group has grown alongside Korean industry across finance, manufacturing and services. DB World leads the group’s real estate and leisure business.

Starting as Miryung Construction in 1969, the group expanded into steel, materials, logistics and finance on the strength of its success in Middle East construction in the 1970s. Renamed from Dongbu to DB Group in 2017, its member companies share the core value of trust across insurance, finance, semiconductors, IT and real estate.
