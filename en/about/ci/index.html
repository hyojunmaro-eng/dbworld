Corporate Identity

DB World follows the corporate identity system of DB Group. The DB symbol expresses trust (green), passion (red) and the future (blue) in harmony.

Symbol Mark

The visual emblem of DB Group and
the centerpiece of all its communications.

Shaped from the letters 'DB', the symbol creatively reinterprets the tradition and spirit of Dongbu for a new era.

Orange and green carry on Dongbu's heritage, while blue — the color of 'east' and 'youth' — expresses hope and the will toward the future.

Sun 태양The energy of passion and challenge
Water 물Hope and the will toward the future
Life 생명New value born where sun meets water

The sun (orange) meets water (blue) to give birth to life (green).

Usage Principles

  • The symbol mark and logotype must not be modified.
  • DB Green is the primary color; gradations are used only at specified ratios.
